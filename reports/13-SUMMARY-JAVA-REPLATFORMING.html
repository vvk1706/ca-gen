1. Executive Summary

The DLIS (Driver License Issuance System) microservices application has been systematically replatformed from Quarkus 3.x to Open Liberty 24.0.0.3 (Jakarta EE 10 / MicroProfile 6.1). The new implementation is located in java-modernisation-liberty/, ensuring that the original code in java-modernisation/ remains completely untouched.

4. Replatforming Highlights

  • Zero Business Logic Loss: All business constraints, fee evaluations, and CA Gen action block rules were preserved exactly.
  • Standardized CDI & JAX-RS: Complete alignment with Jakarta EE 10 specifications.
  • OpenShift on IBM Z Support: Multi-arch Dockerfiles and OpenShift manifests supporting s390x (zLinux) with full x86-64 lift-and-shift capability.
  • Comprehensive Health Monitoring: Configured MicroProfile Health probes for liveness and readiness database validation.

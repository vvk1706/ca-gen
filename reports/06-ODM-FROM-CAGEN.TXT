IBM ODM Knowledge
IBM ODM is a business rules management system (BRMS) that allows you to capture, automate, and govern business decisions. Its key components are:

Decision Center — business rule repository and governance
Rule Execution Server (RES) — runtime engine for executing rules
Decision Model and Notation (DMN) — standard for modeling decisions
Business Action Language (BAL) — natural-language-style rule authoring
Rule Projects — contain rule artifacts (BOM, ruleflows, decision tables, rule templates)
CA Gen → ODM Migration Concepts
CA Gen Concept	ODM Equivalent

Conditions / IF-THEN logic	Business Rules (BAL)
Decision Tables	ODM Decision Tables
Data Views / Entities	Business Object Model (BOM)
Procedure Steps	Rule Tasks in a Ruleflow
Error handling / validation	Rule Agenda / Priority rules
